\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
VTT benchmarks vehicle-attribute recognition across unseen car models. Powertrain macro-F1 reaches 92.6--93.0, while adapted size training improves bi-GRU from 62.9 to 67.1. Deceleration and steering provide physical cues; footprint boundaries and fleet coverage remain priorities for size recognition.

Applications include class-specific traffic summaries, microsimulation calibration and electrified-fleet analysis, extending USDOT/FHWA work~\cite{ban2013mobile,hale2021calibration}. Future work will broaden coverage, refine size labels and evaluate roadside and onboard trajectory inputs.
