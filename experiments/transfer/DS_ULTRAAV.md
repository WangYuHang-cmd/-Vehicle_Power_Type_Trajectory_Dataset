# ULTra-AV adapter: `experiments/transfer/ds_ultraav.py`

Zero-shot transfer set built from the named-vehicle step3 subset of ULTra-AV (CATS Lab, figshare article 26339512 v4,
CC0 with a research-only request; the OpenACC campaigns upstream are CC BY 4.0). Output under
`<data-root>/external/ultraav/work/`: `windows.npz` + `meta.parquet` (the window contract of
`common.py`), `episodes.parquet` (one row per source episode with its raw-run match), `build_report.json`,
`build_tables.md` (the tables below, regenerated by every build) and `windows_summary.json`.

```
source env.sh && $CPY experiments/transfer/ds_ultraav.py              # default build (~70 s, < 2 GB RAM)
$CPY experiments/transfer/ds_ultraav.py --validate-only              # re-run the checks on the saved set
$CPY experiments/transfer/score.py --dataset ultraav --variant <longitudinal-only variant>   # scoring (next step)
```

Options: `--standstill-v` (default 0.1 m/s), `--acc-smooth-s` (0.5 s), `--mhev-as hev|ice` (label of the two 48-V mild
hybrids), `--upsample-5hz` (adds the MicroSimACC Corolla), `--include-ambiguous` (adds the Vicolungo Peugeot without a
footprint), `--no-match` (ignore the raw OpenACC files), `--dataset NAME` (write elsewhere), `--align run|drive`.

## 1. Result in one paragraph

**2,976 windows (49.6 h) from 20 car models, 22 vehicles and 121 drives**, all at native 10 Hz, every candidate block
kept (no gap or standstill drops). Powertrain task: **1,330 BEV windows (6 models, 7 vehicles) vs 657 ICE (5 models)**,
989 windows of 9 further named models carry HEV / PHEV / FCEV / unknown labels and serve the size task only. Size task:
**919 SMALL (8 models) / 944 MID (7 models, 9 vehicles) / 1,113 LARGE (5 models)**; 2,408 windows remain when the three
borderline models (Audi A4 Avant 4.409, Audi e-tron 4.841, Jaguar I-Pace 4.917 m^2) are set aside. Speeds 0.10-41.2 m/s
(mean 13.9), 99.8 % of the samples moving, 97.5 % of the samples with the driving automation engaged (73 windows are
human-driven: 72 from three manual ZalaZone runs recovered from the raw headers, 1 from the Ohio Ford Fusion), a lead
vehicle on 99.999 % of the samples (ULTra-AV is a car-following set), median time headway 1.72 s, median gap 21.1 m.
Every 10 Hz OpenACC-derived episode (1,191 of 1,198) was matched back to its raw JRC run file by exact equality of the
speed sequence, which verified the ID_FAV -> vehicle table of the README (the Casale ids are inverted there) and provided
the per-sample Human / ACC driver flag. The nine lateral / pedal / torque channels do not exist in ULTra-AV: the set must
be scored with the longitudinal-only model variant (CANDIDATES.md 2.3) or labelled "reduced input".

## 2. Channel mapping

| training channel | ULTra-AV source | status | detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| `v_mps` | `Speed_FAV` | measured | GNSS Doppler (OpenACC: OxTS RT-Range at AstaZero, VBOX / u-blox fused B-spline at ZalaZone, u-blox 8/9 cubic spline from 3-5 Hz at Vicolungo / Casale), OBD / GNSS at CATS, lidar-GNSS at Ohio; rows below 0.1 m/s were removed by the authors |
| `a_mps2` | `Speed_FAV` | derived | `common.smooth_derivative` (central difference, 0.5-s moving average). `Acc_FAV` is the raw forward difference and is not used; its 0.5-s mean differs from ours by RMSE 0.01-0.07 m/s^2. Roughness std(diff a) 0.02-0.07 vs 0.15 for the lab's aEgo (the lab's own smoothed derivative gives 0.045): the external accelerations are smoother than training |
| `standstill` | `Speed_FAV` | derived (constant 0) | `v < 0.1 m/s`. Measured on the training tensors, the car's standstill flag is 1 for 97.5 % of the samples below 0.1 m/s, 1.5 % at 0.1-0.3 m/s and 0 % above 0.3 m/s; since ULTra-AV has no rows below 0.1 m/s the lab-equivalent flag is 0 everywhere. The task text's `v < 1 m/s` (`--standstill-v 1.0`) would flag 0.2 % of the window samples with a state that never occurs in training |
| `das_enabled` | raw OpenACC run (`Driver_k` per sample, else the run header `ACC`) / protocol | measured (1,191 episodes) or protocol (CATS, Ohio, 7 unmatched) | AstaZero, Vicolungo, Casale: per-sample `ACC` -> 1, `Human` -> 0 (every matched sample is ACC: the ULTra-AV authors removed the manual laps and samples themselves); ZalaZone has no per-sample flag, the run header gives 1 (ACC) or 0 (three manual handling runs, 72 windows); CATS-ACC / CATS-UWM: 1 (stock ACC by protocol); Ohio Fusion: 0 (manual) |
| `lead_valid` | `Spatial_Gap > 0.5 m` | derived | lab rule (`LEAD_VALID_DREL_MIN_M`); 220 of 2.2 M samples fall below (0.01-0.5 m gaps left by the authors' cleaning); all six lead channels are NaN there |
| `lead_d_m` | `Spatial_Gap` | measured | bumper-to-bumper distance (centre distance minus half lengths, 4.5 m per pair at OpenACC), capped at 120 m by the authors (training clip 200 m) |
| `lead_v_rel_mps` | `Speed_Diff` | measured | = `Speed_LV - Speed_FAV` exactly (max deviation 1e-14), lead minus ego = the training sign |
| `lead_speed_mps` | `v + v_rel` (= `Speed_LV`) | measured | `common.lead_channels` |
| `lead_a_slope_mps2` | lead speed | derived | causal 1-s least-squares slope inside the lead episode, >= 3 samples, clipped +-4 (`common.lead_channels`); NaN on 0.09 % of the lead samples (episode starts) |
| `thw_s` | gap / v | derived | v > 0.5 m/s and gap > 0.5 m (lab rule) |
| `ttc_s` | gap / closing speed | derived | closing > 0.1 m/s (lab rule); large values are fine, the scorer clips at 60 s |
| `steer_angle_deg`, `steer_rate_degps`, `steer_torque_norm`, `yaw_rate_radps`, `curv_1pm`, `lat_acc_mps2`, `gas_pressed`, `brake_pressed`, `steer_pressed` | - | missing (NaN) | not recorded by ULTra-AV; outside the training augmentation -> longitudinal-only variant |

Window rule: `common.cut_windows(align="run")` on every episode (600 samples, no gap > 0.3 s, >= 20 % moving); the
episodes are gap-free by construction (dt = 0.100 s everywhere), so every 600-sample block from the episode start is a
window and the trailing partial block is dropped. `meta.t0_s` is the episode-relative start time, `w_idx` counts the
windows of a drive.

Identifiers: `vehicle_id = "<source> <model>"` (one physical car per model and campaign; the two CATS Lincoln MKZs are
`... 2016` / `... 2017`), `drive_id = "<source>/<raw run file>"` for matched episodes (one raw run = one contiguous
recording, 10-150 min) and `"<source>/traj<Trajectory_ID>"` for CATS / Ohio episodes (each its own recording). The
Tesla Model 3 of AstaZero (July 2019) and of ZalaZone (October 2019) are two vehicle ids as instructed; they may be the
same physical car (JRC fleet), which only matters for the bootstrap clusters.

## 5. Validation (all passed; `--validate-only` repeats it)

* Shape / dtype / contract: `save_windows` accepted the set without warnings; flags are 0 / 1 only; `channels_present`
  is the same 11-channel list for every window.
* No NaN inside the declared channels: `v_mps, a_mps2, standstill, das_enabled, lead_valid` have none; `lead_d_m,
  lead_v_rel_mps, lead_speed_mps` are finite exactly where `lead_valid = 1`; `thw_s` and `ttc_s` follow the lab rules
  (checked with a 1e-5 tolerance at the thresholds because the stored channels are float32); `lead_speed = v + v_rel`
  to 1e-3; the nine absent channels are NaN everywhere.
* Physical ranges: v 0.10-41.2 m/s; |a| 99th percentile 1.74, maximum 4.74 m/s^2 (training clip 8); gap 0.5-120 m;
  |v_rel| <= 30 m/s; thw and ttc positive; lead slope within +-4.
* Moving share 99.8 % of the samples (every window >= 99 % moving in the per-model means); standstill share 0.
* Label consistency: one model / size class / powertrain per vehicle id; the raw vehicle name of every matched episode
  equals the label table; lead speed and gap of the matched stretches equal the raw leader's `Speed` and `IVS` columns
  (0 mismatches), confirming the leader pairing.
* das_enabled: measured on 1,191 episodes (raw per-sample Driver flag at AstaZero / Vicolungo / Casale, run header at
  ZalaZone), protocol on 469; 73 human-driven windows.
* Scorer smoke test: `score.py --dataset ultraav` ran end-to-end (both final sets, probabilities and reports written to
  `work/`); with the lateral channels absent the full-input models are out of distribution there (size GRU 28.8 %
  window accuracy vs 37.4 % majority; BEV/ICE ResNet 45.4 % vs 66.9 % majority), which is the expected reason for the
  longitudinal-only variant of CANDIDATES.md 2.3, not a property of the adapter.

## 6. Decisions and caveats

1. **standstill** is `v < 0.1 m/s` (constant 0 here) rather than the `v < 1 m/s` of the task text: the lab flag is the
   vehicle's "wheels stopped" bit (97.5 % at v < 0.1, 0 % at v >= 0.3 m/s in the training tensors) and ULTra-AV removed
   every row below 0.1 m/s, so slow rolling must not be flagged. `--standstill-v 1.0` reproduces the task wording.
2. **Mild hybrids**: Audi A4 Avant and Mercedes GLE 450 are 48-V MHEVs. The OpenACC spec files list them HEV and
   CANDIDATES.md excludes them from the powertrain score; the lab convention (RAM eTorque, Focus MHEV = ICE) and
   `specs_external.csv` call them ICE. Default label HEV (= excluded from BEV / ICE, used for size); `--mhev-as ice`
   flips both. The Mazda 3 (24-V M Hybrid) and Audi A6 40 TDI (12-V starter-generator) stay ICE as in the spec files
   and CANDIDATES.md.
3. **Casale** ID_FAV 0 is the Hyundai Nexo (the raw order is Rexton -> Hyundai in all nine runs, the matched follower
   column is the Hyundai); the README table is inverted. FCEV -> size task only.
4. **Manual driving inside an "automated vehicle" set**: the ULTra-AV authors removed the manual AstaZero laps and the
   manual Vicolungo samples (the matched samples are all ACC) but kept three manual ZalaZone handling runs (no
   per-sample flag there): 72 windows now carry `das_enabled = 0`. Scoring may compare them with the lab's human-only
   protocol; everything else is ACC-driven and should be compared with the automation-engaged windows.
5. **Excluded cars**: Vanderbilt SUV (unnamed), Ohio Tesla sedan (model undisclosed, automation state unknown), the
   Peugeot (3008 in the raw headers vs 5008 in the spec file; `--include-ambiguous` adds its 9 ICE windows for the
   powertrain task only), the 1 Hz CATS platoon file, the 5 Hz MicroSimACC Corolla (`--upsample-5hz`: linear upsampling
   flagged in `note`, at most 5 windows).
6. **a_mps2 is smoother than training** (std of the first difference 0.02-0.07 vs 0.15 m/s^2 for aEgo): spline-interpolated
   GNSS speed at Vicolungo / Casale / ZalaZone and a 0.5-s moving average. The longitudinal profile is the ACC
   controller's, not a driver's; ZalaZone is a low-speed (30-60 km/h) test-track campaign, Casale a 110-140 km/h
   highway run, so speed distributions differ by campaign and the vehicles are confounded with campaigns (within-campaign
   comparisons: AstaZero Model 3 vs A6 / X5 / A-Class on identical laps is the cleanest).
7. **One car per model** (two Lincoln MKZs and, formally, two Model 3s): the vehicle-cluster bootstrap has 22 clusters;
   per-model reporting is the honest level. The Model 3 of AstaZero and ZalaZone may be one physical car.
8. **Lead channels** are complete and radar-free (differential / fused GNSS of both cars): `lead_a_slope` comes from the
   leader's own speed, which is cleaner than the radar-derived training slope; `lead_valid` is 1 on 99.99 % of the
   samples whereas training has 56 %.
9. The 10 Hz rows of Vicolungo / Casale are cubic splines of 3-5 Hz GNSS, ZalaZone 2nd-degree B-splines of fused sensors,
   AstaZero true 10 Hz samples of a 100 Hz RT-Range stream; only AstaZero matches the training's "samples, not means"
   convention exactly.
10. Nothing in `build`, `release`, `private` or the raw library was touched; the raw ULTra-AV and OpenACC folders are
    read-only inputs; no device or route identifiers exist in this source (public dataset indices and campaign file
    names only appear in `episodes.parquet` / `drive_id`).
